Digital provenance systems can improve accountability in AI-assisted research workflows (Kaudo, 2024). Researchers increasingly use generative AI across literature review, drafting and analysis, but many current workflows do not preserve an inspectable record of where machine assistance occurred. Provenance mechanisms always eliminate research misconduct. One recent study reported that provenance tools significantly increased reviewers' confidence in research outputs (Uncaptured, 2025).
